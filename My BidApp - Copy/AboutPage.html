About TheBid

Your Gaming Marketplace and Auction Platform

About The Project

TheBid is a web-based gaming marketplace designed to provide gamers with a convenient platform where they can browse games, purchase gaming equipment, manage their shopping cart, participate in auctions, and maintain a personal user profile. The system combines online shopping and competitive bidding into a single user-friendly application.

The website was developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Local Storage technology to create an interactive experience without requiring a database connection. Users can log in, explore products, add items to their cart, update their profile information, place bids on auction items, and complete purchases through the checkout process.

What Our Website Offers

  • Secure user login system
  • Personalized user profiles
  • Browse and search gaming products
  • Gaming equipment marketplace
  • Shopping cart management
  • Order checkout and confirmation system
  • Auction and bidding functionality
  • Product category filtering
  • Contact and feedback support page
  • Responsive design for different devices

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a modern online destination for gamers where they can easily purchase gaming products, discover new games, and participate in exciting auctions for rare gaming collectibles. We aim to provide a seamless, engaging, and enjoyable experience for gaming enthusiasts of all levels.

How The System Works

After logging in, users can browse available games and gaming equipment. Products can be added to a shopping cart and purchased through the checkout system. Users may also participate in auctions by placing bids that exceed the current highest bid. Profile information can be updated and stored locally, while cart data is maintained using browser Local Storage for a smooth shopping experience.

Technologies Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript (ES6)
  • Browser Local Storage
  • Responsive Web Design Principles
